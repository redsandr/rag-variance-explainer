# Financial research domain

Language for the analyst workflow. These terms describe the product's meaning, not an implemented feature list.

## Language

**Research question**:
A question about a company's reported performance, risks, or a potential decision requiring evidence and uncertainty to be stated.

**Filing evidence**:
A passage or reported figure in an identified filing, tied to its company, reporting period, and original source.

**Uploaded-document evidence**:
A passage or figure from a user's document, tied to that document and its location; it is not automatically a regulatory filing.

**News evidence**:
A dated, attributed report about an event or assertion. It can add context or corroboration but cannot retroactively establish what a company reported in a filing.

**Analyst finding**:
A conclusion that distinguishes reported facts, checkable calculations, interpretation, and unresolved questions.
_Avoid_: verified answer, when only a quote has matched a retrieved passage.

**Decision consideration**:
A conditional option or next check for an analyst, with supporting evidence, counterevidence, and uncertainty; it is not a buy/sell instruction.
_Avoid_: recommendation, when no suitability assessment has been performed.

**Abstention**:
An explicit refusal to infer a finding when the available evidence is missing, inconsistent, or cannot be checked.
