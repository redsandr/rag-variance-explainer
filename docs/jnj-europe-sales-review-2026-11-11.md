## Owner-approved English walkthrough copy

**Question:** What drove the change in Johnson & Johnson's Europe sales in fiscal Q1 2026?

**Finding:** J&J reported that sales by its companies in Europe grew **14.5%** versus fiscal Q1 2025. It attributed **11.8 percentage points** of that growth to positive currency impact and **2.7 percentage points** to operational growth. Currency was the larger reported component.

**Filing quotation:** “In the fiscal first quarter of 2026, sales by companies in Europe achieved growth of 14.5%, which included operational growth of 2.7% and a positive currency impact of 11.8%.”

**Source:** [J&J's original Form 10-Q, Item 2, Analysis of consolidated sales](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000087/jnj-20260329.htm).

**What this can and cannot tell you:** This is a company-reported regional growth breakdown, not an independently calculated sales bridge, proof of other business causes, or investment advice. A matching quotation alone does not establish that all generated claims are correct; this narrow copy was separately checked against the filing.
