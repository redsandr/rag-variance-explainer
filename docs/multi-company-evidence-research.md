# Multi-company evidence gap: source review and proposed evaluation

Status: candidate code change with non-model checks; **end-to-end outcome unverified**. Date: 2026-11-11. No embedding, reranker, NLI, or answer-generation model was run in this investigation.

## What the operator run demonstrates

The 270-character CMG/JNJ question requested two different metrics and periods. Five retrieved passages included CMG chunks 6, 3, 7 (period 2024-06-30) and JNJ chunks 10, 12 (period 2026-03-29). The final answer correctly gave a CMG revenue-share direction but said JNJ Europe sales growth was not available **in the provided context**. Only one structured claim (CMG) was quoted and passed NLI; this is not evidence that the JNJ portion was answered. The output displayed “Research finding” and “Quote matched in retrieved text,” which may wrongly imply full coverage. See [operator log](operator-rag-smoke-test-2026-11-11.md).

A read-only Chroma `get(ids=...)` of the two cited JNJ chunks found no `14.5` substring in either. Chunk 12 mentions Europe only in an unrelated tax passage; chunk 10 has no `Europe` mention. JNJ **chunk 1** from the same accession contains: “In the fiscal first quarter of 2026, sales by companies in Europe achieved growth of 14.5%, which included operational growth of 2.7% and a positive currency impact of 11.8%.” This text also appeared in the operator's earlier single-company JNJ answer and can be checked against the [original JNJ filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000087/jnj-20260329.htm). The indexed target span exists, but **the multi-company top-five passages omitted it**. Do not infer the LLM ignored evidence it never saw.

## Actual code path

- `src/retrieval.py::query_multi()` now reserves per-company slots when both entities and their period mapping are explicit, but each scoped search still uses the **entire multi-topic question** for query expansion, dense retrieval, BM25, and cross-encoder reranking. Slot reservation establishes company coverage, **not relevant-metric coverage**. Changing only `top_k` may displace other needed passages and is not a general fix.
- `src/rag.py::answer_question()` checks that each generated claim's quote occurs in its cited retrieved passage. It does not require each requested company/metric to have a sourced claim; `parsed.answer` is separate prose and can assert missing information while only the CMG claim is verified. NLI examines only generated claims, so `nli_claim=0` cannot adjudicate an omitted JNJ answer.
- The logged JNJ statement was qualified “in the provided context”: literally consistent with the passages selected, but misleading if surfaced as evidence of absence from the **filing**. Distinguish *not indexed*, *indexed but not retrieved*, *retrieved but not used*, and *actually absent in reviewed filing*.

## Research consulted and boundaries

- [IRCoT, Trivedi et al. (ACL 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10509): reports that a one-step retrieve-and-read approach can be inadequate for multi-step QA and studies interleaved retrieval on other datasets. It motivates testing question decomposition; it does **not** show that a deterministic parser or any particular retriever will work for SEC financial questions.
- [Ragas, Es et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15217): distinguishes retrieval relevance/focus, use of evidence by the generator, and generation quality. Its reference-free framework does **not** independently certify the missing-evidence labels for this application. Measure retrieval target-span recall separately from final-answer coverage.
- [Lost in the Middle, Liu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172): models can underuse relevant information depending on long-context placement. **Not the cause demonstrated here**: the target JNJ passage was missing from the prompt entirely. Revisit only if the passage is later present but ignored.
- [FActScore, Min et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251) motivates checking atomic answer facts; [FEVER, Thorne et al.](https://aclanthology.org/N18-1074/) uses supported/refuted/not-enough-info with annotated evidence. Their non-SEC results must not be transferred as app accuracy claims. They motivate evaluating omitted sub-answers and unsupported absence statements separately.
- [Official Chroma metadata filtering](https://docs.trychroma.com/docs/querying-collections/metadata-filtering) documents `where` for ticker/period prefilters; those prefilters are functioning in this operator run. [Chroma upstream issue #1394](https://github.com/chroma-core/chroma/issues/1394) and [reopened performance concern #7092](https://github.com/chroma-core/chroma/issues/7092) are community reports about metadata-filter latency at other scales/versions, **not** evidence of the missing JNJ metric or measured slowdown here.
- A general web-search request returned HTTP 402. GitHub API searches for an exact, applicable community resolution to multi-company **metric** coverage yielded no verified case. An examined [LangChain MultiQueryRetriever issue #17352](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langchain/issues/17352) concerns a parser error, not this omission; it is deliberately **not** used as supporting evidence. No forum consensus or drop-in fix was found.

## Synthesis to test, not a decision to ship

1. Build independently reviewed target-span cases for several indexed companies and periods (including two companies with different requested metrics, same-metric comparisons, missing scopes, conflicting dates, and absent facts). Keep development and untouched holdout questions separate. Record the SEC original span and indexed chunk ID. The current one example is a regression trigger, not a tuning set.
2. On the development set, compare current full-question/per-scope retrieval against a **scoped-question** candidate: for each explicit company/period, retain the clause specifying that company's metric and time period rather than searching both metrics together. Validate the parsed plan; ambiguous relationships should request clarification rather than guess. Measure target-span recall@allocated-k and latency, including failures, before choosing a policy. Do not force in JNJ chunk 1 by ID.
3. Independently add an answer-coverage check for multi-part questions: every requested scope needs a supported sourced claim **or an explicit, accurately qualified abstention**. If a target span was not retrieved, do not tell the user the SEC filing lacks the fact. A check that merely counts mentioned ticker strings would not establish metric support. Test quote uniqueness, arithmetic, causality, and negative evidence separately; do not use NLI's positive verdict as proof of coverage.
4. The operator runs model-dependent retrieval and generation comparisons after non-model tests pass. Report stage-specific results and raw per-question outputs; do not claim a successful fix until the holdout and original-filing review support it.

## Candidate implementation (pending operator model evaluation)

`src/retrieval.py` now narrows each per-company lookup to the segment starting at that company's explicit name and ending at its **one unambiguous local report date**, only when the report date matches the company-period plan. If a segment has no date or multiple dates, it retains the original question rather than guessing an association. Ticker/period metadata prefilters and per-company result allocations remain intact. Deterministic tests reproduced the prior behavior where the JNJ lookup still contained CMG labor costs, then verified the scoped JNJ lookup contains Europe sales but not CMG labor costs. These mocked tests do **not** establish that the live embedding and reranker will select the JNJ target span.

`src/rag.py` now refuses to display a structured multi-company answer as a sourced finding if no **quote-validated claim** cites a passage for each requested company-period. It gives an explicit incomplete-coverage reason; earlier quote-validation errors still take precedence. This is a **minimum coverage gate**, not semantic verification of the cited metric: an irrelevant claim about JNJ could pass the scope check. Numerical arithmetic, units, causes, multi-metric coverage within one company, and missing-evidence assertions still require independent checks. Tests cover one-claim refusal and two-company acceptance with mock LLM outputs; no AI model ran here.

Next release check: operator reruns the *same* CMG/JNJ question on Streamlit with All tickers and captures the five source IDs, full answer, quote matches, and relevant diagnostics. Require JNJ's **Europe-sales** span—not just a JNJ ticker—and check both parts against the original filings. Test diverse company/metric combinations and timeouts before treating the candidate as general or tuning ranking.
