# Landing surface

Mode: Persuade. Audience: independent financial analysts and small research teams. Primary action: inspect an interactive explanation of the research flow. Proof: only a manually reviewed, filing-backed case can be shown as verified. No signup, price, or public API is promised.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show what the tool helps an analyst investigate before explaining the technology. A visible evidence workpaper and visitor-controlled demonstration replace the automatic terminal story. No fabricated result, quote, or performance chart is used.

OWN-WORLD: Dark charcoal is the default; pale gold marks meaningful interaction, with a visitor-selectable paper mode. Quiet filing-paper surfaces and precise evidence states keep the analysis grounded.

STORY: A first-time analyst understands the use case, inspects a self-paced example without invented filing evidence, then follows dedicated pages for method, local installation, project status, or a case request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A restrained floating navigation frames a direct benefit headline and two actions. Beside the headline a readable sample workpaper shows the slots for an answer, quoted passage, source and review status, explicitly withheld until verified. On mobile the statement leads and the workpaper follows immediately.
