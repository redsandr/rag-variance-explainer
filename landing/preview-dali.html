02The problem

Finding out why a number moved takes hours.

An analyst with a PDF of a 10-K has to find the right paragraph, cross-check the figure, and trust they read it right. Slow, error-prone — and it doesn’t scale across 7 companies and 4 industries.

4 hrs → 3 min
  • The manual crawlOpen the filing, search the filer, find the number, read the context.
  • The trust gapNo guarantee the paragraph you landed on is the one that moved the metric.
  • The scale wallDo this across a basket and the process breaks down completely.

Dense + BM25 + cross-encoder to surface the best match.

04

Answer

Every claim written and cited back to the exact filing page.

07Why it works
  • 01

    Ask like you’d ask a colleague

    No filing jargon required. Plain-English questions, scoped to what you actually meant.

  • 02

    Every claim cited to a page

    Each answer points back to the exact filing and page so you can verify before it goes in a report.

  • 03

    Pulls straight from SEC filings

    Reads directly from 10-Ks and 10-Qs across 7 companies — no manual PDF searching.

  • 04

    Works across sectors

    Tested on restaurant, retail, healthcare, and energy — with no accuracy drop between them.

  • 05

    Checked against the source

    Roughly 3 in 4 answers verified as fully accurate against the original filing text.

  • 06

    Your filings stay yours

    Runs fully offline on your machine, or connects to a cloud provider — your data, your choice.
